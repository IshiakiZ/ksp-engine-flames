Engine Flames @VERSION@
for Kerbal Space Program 1.12.x

A rocket engine's exhaust as burning gas: it leaves the nozzle, is slowed and stirred by the
air, widens, cools from white through yellow to orange, strikes the ground and runs out over
it. Worked out bit by bit and drawn as a true volume, as the Volumetric Explosions mod makes
its fire, in whose air it burns.

INSTALL
  This mod needs two others, each a separate download:
    Keystone 0.9 or later, the base it stands on:  https://spacedock.info/mod/4665/Keystone
    Volumetric Explosions 0.8 or later, which makes and draws the fire:  https://spacedock.info/mod/4669/Volumetric%20Explosions
  Copy their folders (Keystone and VolumetricExplosions) into your KSP's GameData folder,
  and the EngineFlames folder from this download beside them, and start the game. Without both
  the game does not load this mod. One copy of each serves every mod that uses it. To remove
  this mod, delete the EngineFlames folder.

USE
  Nothing to do: every rocket engine has its new flame. Its settings (brightness, length,
  width, and switching it off) are in the Keystone window: the keystone button on the game's
  toolbar, or Option-K on a Mac and Alt-K elsewhere. Then turn to Engine Flames.

BESIDE OTHER MODS
  It works with the Waterfall mod or without it. While one of its flames burns for an engine,
  the flame the game or Waterfall draws for that engine is hidden; the smoke is left (the
  Rocket Smoke mod does for smoke what this does for flames). Jets, ion engines and engines
  that burn something it does not know keep the flames they had.

USE VOLUMETRIC EXPLOSIONS 0.8 OR LATER
  This version's flames (their starts and stops, the afterburner, the glowing bells, the
  storable fuels' clear flame) are drawn by what that version knows.

NO RE-ENTRY FIRE IN 0.5.0
  The re-entry fire of 0.4.0 is taken out while it is made again; until it is back, the
  game's own re-entry streaks show as the game has them.

WHERE IT WORKS
  Made and tried on a Mac, and tried on Windows 11 (all but 0.5.0's starts and stops, which
  have run on the Mac only so far). The flames are drawn by Volumetric
  Explosions' shaders, packed for the game's OpenGL (a Mac, Linux) and built for Direct3D
  (Windows); nobody has run it on Linux yet. Where that mod cannot draw a volume, this one
  says so in its settings and leaves the game's flames as they are.

Everything else: https://github.com/IshiakiZ/ksp-engine-flames
