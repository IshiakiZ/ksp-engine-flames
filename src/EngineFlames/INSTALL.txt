Engine Flames @VERSION@
for Kerbal Space Program 1.12.x

A rocket engine's exhaust as burning gas: it leaves the nozzle, is slowed and stirred by the
air, widens, cools from white through yellow to orange, strikes the ground and runs out over
it. Worked out bit by bit and drawn as a true volume, as the Volumetric Explosions mod makes
its fire, in whose air it burns.

INSTALL
  Copy the three folders in this download (Keystone, VolumetricExplosions and
  EngineFlames) into your KSP's GameData folder, and start the game. Keystone is the base this
  mod stands on, and Volumetric Explosions makes and draws the fire: one copy of each serves
  every mod that uses it. To remove the mod, delete the EngineFlames folder.

USE
  Nothing to do: every rocket engine has its new flame. Its settings (brightness, length,
  width, and switching it off) are in the Keystone window: the keystone button on the game's
  toolbar, or Option-K on a Mac and Alt-K elsewhere. Then turn to Engine Flames.

BESIDE OTHER MODS
  It works with the Waterfall mod or without it. While one of its flames burns for an engine,
  the flame the game or Waterfall draws for that engine is hidden; the smoke is left (the
  Rocket Smoke mod does for smoke what this does for flames). Jets, ion engines and engines
  that burn something it does not know keep the flames they had.

KNOWN FAULT
  The flame does not yet move as fire does: it keeps its line when the ship turns, like a
  rod, and nothing in it is seen to stream. Being reworked.

WHERE IT WORKS
  Made and tried on a Mac, and tried on Windows 11. The flames are drawn by Volumetric
  Explosions' shaders, packed for the game's OpenGL (a Mac, Linux) and built for Direct3D
  (Windows); nobody has run it on Linux yet. Where that mod cannot draw a volume, this one
  says so in its settings and leaves the game's flames as they are.
