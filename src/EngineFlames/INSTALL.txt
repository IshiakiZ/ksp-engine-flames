Engine Flames @VERSION@
for Kerbal Space Program 1.12.x

A rocket engine's exhaust drawn as a true volume of glowing gas: shaped by the air it is in
(a narrow column with shock diamonds at the pad, a soft wide plume in a vacuum), coloured by
what it burns, and spreading over the ground where it strikes it.

INSTALL
  Copy both folders in this download's GameData (Keystone and EngineFlames) into your KSP's
  GameData folder, and start the game. Keystone is the base this mod stands on; one copy
  serves every mod that uses it. To remove the mod, delete the EngineFlames folder.

USE
  Nothing to do: every rocket engine has its new flame. Its settings (brightness, length,
  width, how torn, shock diamonds, flame along the ground, and switching it off) are in the
  Keystone window: the keystone button on the game's toolbar, or Option-K on a Mac and Alt-K
  elsewhere. Then turn to Engine Flames.

BESIDE OTHER MODS
  It works with the Waterfall mod or without it. While one of its flames is drawn for an
  engine, the flame the game or Waterfall draws for that engine is hidden; the smoke is left.
  Jets, ion engines and engines that burn something it does not know keep the flames they had.

WHERE IT WORKS
  Made and tried on a Mac. It is drawn by a shader made for the game's OpenGL, which is what
  the game uses on a Mac and on Linux; nobody has run it on Linux yet. On Windows (Direct3D)
  there is no flame shader yet: the mod says so in its settings and leaves the game's flames
  as they are.
